Langit Nusantara v2 — a realistic 3D flight game: fly the twin-prop Merpati Nusantara through glowing rings over the Indonesian archipelago at golden hour, then land on runway 36 and earn your grade. Six named sorties — mail run, volcano rim, storm rescue, crosswind precision, medevac dusk, island hop — each with its own rings, weather and fuel, picked from a dispatch board with medals and a route chart. Chase cam or lit cockpit (press C), with airspeed, altitude, heading and artificial horizon; every landing is debriefed with an approach-trace graph and a grade S/A/B/C. Three.js, offline, keyboard + mouse.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000 --directory game
# then open http://localhost:8000 in desktop Chrome
```

Controls: arrows/WASD fly, W/S throttle, A/D rudder, Space brake, P pause, C cycles chase / cockpit / near, 1–6 pick a sortie on the dispatch board, R retry a sortie, ESC back to the board. Landings are graded S/A/B/C on sink rate, centerline and bank — the debrief shows the approach trace against the ideal 3° glidepath.
